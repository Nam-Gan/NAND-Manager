Commanding Unit commands the nand master to do operations in a particular sequence. Also handles the dataflow to and from the NAND-Flash.

The busy wire indicates the state of nand_master fsm.

activate must be held high while submitting any command.

Read:
    M_NAND_RESET 8'd1
    Fetch read address of the current sensor from the addressing unit
    MI_SET_CURRENT_ADDRESS 8'd19
    MI_CHIP1_ENABLE/MI_CHIP2_ENABLE 8'd9/8'd24
    M_NAND_READ 8'd6
    MI_CHIP_DISABLE 8'd10
    MI_GET_DATA_PAGE_BYTE 8'd16

Write:
    M_NAND_RESET 8'd1
    Fetch the write address of the current sensor from the  addressing unit
    MI_SET_DATA_PAGE_BYTE 8'd17
    MI_SET_CURRENT_ADDRESS 8'd19
    MI_CHIP1_ENABLE/MI_CHIP2_ENABLE 8'd9/8'd24
    MI_WRITE_ENABLE 8'd12
    M_NAND_PAGE_PROGRAM 8'd7
    MI_WRITE_PROTECT 8'd11
    MI_CHIP_DISABLE 8'd10

Mode Change:
    M_NAND_RESET 8'd1

Addressing Scheme:
20 bits/region (starting page address).
| LUN-select (1 bit) | Plane-select (1 bit) | Block-select (11 bit) | Page-select (7 bit)|
This way incrementing and decrementing becomes easier for the addressing unit.
